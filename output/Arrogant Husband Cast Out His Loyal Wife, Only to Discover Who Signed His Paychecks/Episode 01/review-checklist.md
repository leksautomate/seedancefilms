# Review checklist — Arrogant Husband Cast Out His Loyal Wife, Only to Discover Who Signed His Paychecks, Episode 01

Use this on each generated clip, next to its brief. It records a human review of one clip. A clip that renders is not a clip that passes. (Structure adapted from the SceneVela AI video output review checklist, MIT licensed.)

## For every clip

### 1. The file
- [ ] Plays from start to finish; duration and orientation are right
- [ ] No frozen, blank or duplicated frames
- [ ] Audio is present and not clipped

### 2. Matches the brief
- [ ] Every spoken line is the script's line, in order, in the right voice
- [ ] Hard cuts only: no tracking, slides or whip pans
- [ ] No music
- [ ] Each shot does the one job the brief gave it
- [ ] Every stability constraint holds

### 3. Continuity
- [ ] Each character's design, clothing and proportions match the references and the previous clip
- [ ] Left/right positions and props match the brief's carry-over
- [ ] Lighting and colour match the rest of the scene
- [ ] No unintended text, logo, watermark or extra person
- [ ] The last frame is stable enough to extend from (when an extension follows)

### 4. Rights and disclosure
- [ ] Every reference asset is owned or authorised
- [ ] Any AI-generated-content disclosure the destination requires is ready

## Decision for each clip

ACCEPT: all checks pass. ITERATE: usable, one named change needed. REJECT: misses the brief. PUBLISH HOLD: permission or disclosure still missing.
