# Camera angles for AI video prompts

Source: the video "The Secret of Camera Angles" (8 most used camera angles) plus the extra angle
terms used in the example prompts.

Camera angles define where the viewer is placed in a scene and shape how the moment is experienced.
In AI video prompting, name the angle in the shot heading and again in the framing sentence. Shots are
separated with explicit cuts (the video shows the form `[cut] aerial shot of ...`, `[cut] close up shot of ...`,
`[cut] over the shoulder shot`).

## The 8 core angles

| Angle | What it shows | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Full shot | The entire character and their surroundings | Space, context, movement |
| Medium shot | The character from the waist up | Dialogue scenes, character-focused moments |
| Close-up shot | The face from the shoulders up | Facial expression, emotion, reaction |
| Over-the-shoulder shot | Camera behind the character, showing what they see | Conversations, a character looking at something |
| Low angle shot | The subject from below | Power, dominance, drama |
| Insert shot | A close-up of an object or detail important to the scene | Props, clues, devices (phone, bomb, money, metal ball) |
| Aerial view (bird's-eye) | The scene from high above | Showing everything clearly from a distance, geography, isolation |
| Establishing shot | A wide shot of the setting or location | Opening a scene, moving to a new location, before focusing on characters |

The video's advice: no need to memorize all of them. Start with close-up, over-the-shoulder, insert, and aerial, then add the others.

## Pairing angle with purpose

- Dialogue exchange: medium or medium close-up on the speaker, close-up on the reaction.
- Big reaction or a punchline: tight close-up.
- Introducing a place: establishing or wide shot, then cut in.
- A prop that matters: insert shot, tell the model what is in sharp focus.
- Threat or power: low angle. Helplessness or scale: high angle or aerial.
- A hidden threat: close-up on the reacting character, never on the threat.
