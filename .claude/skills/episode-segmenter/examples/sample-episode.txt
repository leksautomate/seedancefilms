EPISODE 1 - "THE SIX"

SCENE 1 - INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT

WOLF sits at the table assembling a fictional training bomb. A one-sided magnifying lens covers his eye. He handles tiny parts with tweezers.
GIRL stands in front of a full-length mirror, fighting with the button of her jeans.
GIRL: BABE!
WOLF flinches, nearly drops the tweezers, then looks up, annoyed.
GIRL: Do I look fat in these jeans?
WOLF: No.
He goes back to the bomb.
GIRL: Are you SURE? They feel so tight...
WOLF: Then get a bigger size.
GIRL looks offended, eyebrows shooting up.
GIRL: THEY'RE ALREADY A SIX!
WOLF stops working and looks up, genuinely surprised.
WOLF: Six? I thought you were, like, a TEN!
GIRL freezes. Confusion turns to disbelief, then outrage.
GIRL: You thought what?
WOLF puts down the tweezers and removes the magnifying lens, resigned.
WOLF: Here we go...
CUT TO BLACK.

SCENE 2 - INT. BEDROOM - NIGHT

ROXIE scrolls on her phone in bed, then puts on a white LED therapy mask. Her wolf nose sticks out through the nose opening. Red light glows across her fur.
DIESEL
What the hell is that?
ROXIE
Red light therapy.
DIESEL
For what?
ROXIE
Collagen. Better skin. Less wrinkles.
DIESEL
Your entire face is covered in fur.
ROXIE
Better skin means better fur. It's basic science.
DIESEL
That's definitely how fur works, Roxie...
ROXIE rolls her eyes and goes back to scrolling.
ROXIE: Whatever.

SCENE 3 - EXT. UNDERGROUND CITY - NIGHT

A huge layered city of pipes, bridges and waterfalls fades through haze.

SCENE 4 - INT. RESTAURANT - NIGHT

RUFUS wipes the counter after closing, quietly singing. A red landline phone sits beside him. It rings. He stops singing and picks up.
DIESEL (on phone): You done?
RUFUS: Yeah.
DIESEL (on phone): Bring your chopper.
RUFUS: Always do.
RUFUS hangs up. CLICK. A brief ominous pause.

SCENE 5 - EXT. BRIDGE - DAY

ROXIE and DIESEL look up. A small drone hovers over the bridge.
DIESEL: RUN!
They sprint across the bridge. The drone chases from behind and above.
Its targeting interface locks onto both of them. The interface flashes red.
The drone fires repeatedly. Bullets strike the bridge around their feet.
DIESEL: ROXIE!
ROXIE: WHAT?
DIESEL: DROP THE EXIT... NOW!
Roxie reaches into her black bag while running and pulls out a golf-ball-sized gunmetal sphere. Its segments shift as it activates.
She throws the sphere onto the bridge. It hits the ground and unfolds into a glowing platform.
They jump into the platform together. The drone is still chasing them.
They teleport away. The portal closes behind them.
Inside their apartment a wall gadget flares with glowing energy. They crash onto the floor and tumble across it, battered.
DIESEL: Almost.
CUT TO BLACK.
